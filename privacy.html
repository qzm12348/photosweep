Privacy Policy for PhotoSweep

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Zero Collection of Photos or Personal Data

PhotoSweep operates 100% locally inside your browser on https://photos.google.com/*. We do not collect, read, store, transmit, or sell your photos, videos, albums, Google account credentials, browsing history, or personal information.

2. Browser Permissions Explained

  • Host permission (https://photos.google.com/*): Used solely to render the PhotoSweep floating control panel and automate clicks on Google Photos checkboxes and toolbar buttons when you explicitly start a task.
  • storage: Used to store your local preferences (UI language, click delay), daily free quota counter, and Pro license activation status inside chrome.storage.local.
  • activeTab: Used when you click the extension toolbar popup button to open Google Photos or bring the floating panel back into view.

3. License Verification

If you choose to upgrade to PhotoSweep Pro, your license key is validated solely to unlock unlimited usage in your browser. Payment processing is handled securely by our Merchant of Record payment provider; PhotoSweep never sees or stores your credit card or payment details.

4. Safety & Trash Retention

PhotoSweep moves selected items to your Google Photos Trash bin rather than bypassing Trash. Note: If mobile backup & sync is enabled on your phone, deleting cloud photos may also affect synced device copies according to Google Photos' standard behavior.
