Privacy Policy for PhotoSweep

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Zero Collection of Photos or Personal Data

PhotoSweep operates 100% locally inside your browser on https://photos.google.com/*. We do not collect, read, store, transmit, or sell your photos, videos, albums, Google account credentials, browsing history, or personal information.

2. Browser Permissions Explained

  • Host permission (https://photos.google.com/*): Used solely to interact with the Google Photos web interface to automate item selection and batch operations as instructed by you.
  • Storage permission (storage): Used to store your user settings locally (such as batch size, dry-run preferences, and year cutoff filters). No sensitive data is stored.
  • Alarms permission (alarms): Used solely for internal timing and scheduling of cleanup batches.

3. Local-First Processing

All scripts and operations execute entirely in your local browser sandbox. No images, metadata, or browsing activities are ever uploaded to any third-party server, cloud storage, or external database.

4. Third-Party Services and Payments

PhotoSweep is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google LLC. License validation and payments for PhotoSweep Pro are handled securely by merchant partners. No financial information is processed or stored directly by PhotoSweep.

5. Data Security

Because PhotoSweep does not transmit or store personal information externally, your data remains protected under your own Google account security and browser configuration.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@photosweep.app.
